01 · Positioning

From a report people read once to an agent they keep paying.

Today Olmene sells a marketing report. Reports are bought once, ChatGPT deep research makes them look replaceable, and €19 for two of them does not build MRR. The fixer agent already in the codebase changes the category.

Technical founders

02 · Why now

Three shifts make this possible in 2026 and not before.

Shift 1

Code changes got cheap

Coding agents made safe automated edits realistic. The scarce part is knowing which change is worth making. That is Olmene's job.

Shift 2

Discovery moved into AI answers

People now find products through ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI search results. Most founders cannot tell whether those tools can read or cite their site. Olmene checks and fixes it.

Shift 3

Tiny teams, no growth function

One to five person software companies ship like large teams but cannot afford a growth hire. Olmene is that hire at €49 a month.

04 · Defensibility

The dataset nobody else has: which change moved which number.

With read-only Search Console and GA4 connected, every merged PR becomes a measured experiment. Over time Olmene learns which fixes work for which kind of site, and its recommendations get better with every customer. This replaces the current "we never see your analytics" line, so it stays opt-in and clearly explained.

Won't Copilot, Vercel or ChatGPT just do this?

They write code when a person says what to change. Olmene decides what to change, proves it worked, and owns the outcome data. Hosting platforms are more likely to partner with Olmene or acquire it than build it.

Why would a founder let an AI into their repo?

Nothing merges without their approval. Access is limited to one repository. Every change is logged and reversible, and there is a kill switch. This is already built and covered by 58 tests.

Isn't this just another SEO tool?

SEO tools produce to-do lists that nobody finishes. Olmene ships the changes. Its core metric is merged PRs with measured results, not reports generated.

Where does growth come from?

Product-led outreach (free audit plus a free fix PR), public teardowns, build-in-public content, and later one-click installs from hosting marketplaces.

06 · Design direction

The living instrument.

Keep the elm identity and push it toward precision equipment: warm dark surfaces, a faint measurement grid, mono readouts for anything numeric, and green light reserved for one meaning, which is the agent working or a result confirmed. The flower is the agent's heartbeat. It breathes while Olmene works and stays still when it waits for you.

Color

Green means proof

Leaf green marks agent activity and verified results only. Rust marks problems. Amber marks events and example data. Everything else stays neutral.

Motion

One orchestrated moment per screen

Each screen gets a single signature motion (scan beam, diff sweep, line draw) and quiet ambient states elsewhere. Transform and opacity only, 60fps, and every animation turns off under reduced-motion settings.
